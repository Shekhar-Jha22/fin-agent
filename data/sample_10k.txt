APPLE INC. - FORM 10-K EXCERPT (FISCAL YEAR 2023)
UNITED STATES SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION
Washington, D.C. 20549

BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories worldwide. The Company also sells a variety of related services. In fiscal 2023, the Company's net sales were $383.3 billion, compared to $394.3 billion in fiscal 2022. The decrease in net sales reflected lower net sales of iPhone, Mac, and iPad, partially offset by higher net sales of Services and Wearables, Home and Accessories.

The Company manages its business primarily on a geographic basis. The Company's reportable segments consist of the Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, and Rest of Asia Pacific. The Americas segment includes both North and South America. The Europe segment includes European countries, as well as India, the Middle East, and Africa. The Greater China segment includes China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan. The Rest of Asia Pacific segment includes Australia and those Asian countries not included in the Company's other reportable segments.

RISK FACTORS

The Company faces significant competition in each of the markets in which it competes. The markets for the Company's products and services are highly competitive and subject to rapid technological change. The Company's competitors who sell mobile devices and personal computers based on other operating systems have aggressively cut prices and lowered their product margins to gain or maintain market segment share. The Company's financial condition and operating results can be adversely affected by these and other competitive pressures.

Global and regional economic conditions could materially adversely affect the Company. The Company has international operations with sales outside the United States representing approximately 57% of total net sales for fiscal 2023. In addition, the Company's global supply chain is large and complex and a significant portion of the Company's supply chain is located outside of the United States.

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

For fiscal year 2023:
- Total net sales: $383.3 billion
- iPhone net sales: $200.6 billion (52.3% of total)
- Services net sales: $85.2 billion (22.2% of total)
- Mac net sales: $29.4 billion (7.7% of total)
- iPad net sales: $28.3 billion (7.4% of total)
- Wearables, Home and Accessories: $39.8 billion (10.4% of total)

Gross margin for fiscal 2023 was 44.1%, compared to 43.3% in fiscal 2022. The increase in gross margin was primarily due to a different product mix, partially offset by the weakness in foreign currencies relative to the U.S. dollar.

LIQUIDITY AND CAPITAL RESOURCES

The Company believes its existing balances of cash, cash equivalents, and unrestricted marketable securities, together with cash generated by operations and access to debt markets, will be sufficient to satisfy its cash requirements and capital return program over the next 12 months.

As of September 30, 2023, the Company had $61.6 billion in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities, of which $39.0 billion was held by foreign subsidiaries. The Company generated $110.5 billion in cash from operating activities during fiscal 2023.

The Company returned over $89 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases during fiscal 2023. The Company repurchased $77.6 billion of its common stock and paid dividends of $15.0 billion.

SEGMENT INFORMATION

Americas: Net sales of $162.1 billion, down 4% year-over-year
Europe: Net sales of $94.3 billion, down 1% year-over-year
Greater China: Net sales of $72.6 billion, down 2% year-over-year
Japan: Net sales of $24.3 billion, down 7% year-over-year
Rest of Asia Pacific: Net sales of $30.0 billion, up 3% year-over-year

OUTLOOK AND STRATEGY

The Company continues to invest heavily in research and development to drive future innovation. R&D expenses were $29.9 billion in fiscal 2023, up 14% from $26.3 billion in fiscal 2022. The Company believes that continued investment in R&D is critical to its future growth and competitive position in the marketplace.

Apple's Services segment, which includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple TV+, Apple Arcade, and Apple Pay, continues to demonstrate strong growth momentum. The Company expects Services to continue to be a key driver of growth as it expands its installed base and increases monetization.

The Company is committed to becoming carbon neutral across its entire business, manufacturing supply chain, and product life cycle by 2030. Apple has made significant progress on this goal, with 100% of its operations powered by renewable energy since 2018.
